...FLOOD ADVISORY IN EFFECT UNTIL 930 PM EDT THIS EVENING...
...REPLACES FLOOD WARNING...

* WHAT...Flooding caused by excessive runoff is expected.

* WHERE...Henderson County, North Carolina.

* WHEN...Until 930 PM EDT Saturday.

* IMPACTS...Minor flooding in low-lying and poor drainage areas. 
  Water over roadways. River or stream flows are elevated.

* ADDITIONAL DETAILS...
  - At 949 AM EDT, emergency management reported continuing flood
    impacts along Mud and Clear Creeks in and around the city of
    Hendersonville. Mud Creek was reported to still be out of its
    banks. As much as 20 inches of rain fell between Wednesday
    morning and Friday afternoon in Henderson County. Runoff will
    continue but the rivers will slowly recede and should fall
    below flood stage by tonight.
  - Some locations that may experience flooding include...
    Hendersonville, Mills River, Fletcher, Flat Rock, Etowah,
    Mountain Home, Laurel Park, Saluda, Dupont State Forest,
    Jones Gap State Park, Bat Cave, Chimney Rock State Park,
    Valley Hill, East Flat Rock, Horse Shoe, Crab Creek and Dana.
  - http://www.weather.gov/safety/flood

PRECAUTIONARY/PREPAREDNESS ACTIONS...

Stay away or be swept away. River banks and culverts can become 
unstable and unsafe.
