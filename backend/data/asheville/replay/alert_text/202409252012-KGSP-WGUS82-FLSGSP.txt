...FLOOD ADVISORY IN EFFECT UNTIL 6 PM EDT THIS EVENING...

* WHAT...Flooding caused by excessive rainfall is expected.

* WHERE...Portions of northeast Georgia, including the following 
  county, Rabun and upstate South Carolina, including the following 
  county, Oconee.

* WHEN...Until 600 PM EDT.

* IMPACTS...River or stream flows are elevated. 

* ADDITIONAL DETAILS...
  - At 409 PM EDT, Doppler radar and automated rain gauges
    indicated heavy rain due to thunderstorms. Minor flooding is
    ongoing or expected to begin shortly in the advisory area
    with localized rainfall totals around 3 inches in the advised
    area today.  Monitor for elevated flows and/or out of bank

    as Warwoman Creek and the Chauga River.
  - Some locations that may experience flooding include...
    Westminster, Sky Valley, Tallulah Falls, Oconee State Park,
    Tallulah Gorge State Park, Lake Hartwell, Long Creek,
    Mountain Rest and Pine Mountain.
