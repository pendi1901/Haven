Rutherford NC-
745 AM EDT Thu Sep 26 2024

...FLOOD ADVISORY IN EFFECT UNTIL 11 AM EDT THIS MORNING...

* WHAT...Flooding caused by excessive rainfall is expected.

* WHERE...A portion of western North Carolina, including the 
  following county, Rutherford.

* WHEN...Until 1100 AM EDT.

* IMPACTS...Water over roadways possible. 

* ADDITIONAL DETAILS...
  - At 741 AM EDT, Gauge reports were indicating ongoing elevated
    flows throughout the Upper Broad River basin.  High river and
    stream levels will continue all day and when heavy rain
    redevelops, flash flood warnings may be needed. Watch for
    flooding of low-lying areas downstream of the Lake Lure Dam
    and select tributaries.  Water levels will also be rising
    along the Second Broad River so watch for developing above
    bankfull conditions in the basin as well.
  - Some locations that will experience flooding include...
    Forest City, Rutherfordton, Spindale, Lake Lure, Ellenboro,
    Bostic, Chimney Rock Village, South Mountains State Park,
    Chimney Rock State Park, Bat Cave, Alexander Mills, Union
    Mills, Sunshine, Green Hill, Thermal City, Harris, Shingle
    Hollow, Caroleen, Henrietta and Cliffside.
  - http://www.weather.gov/safety/flood

PRECAUTIONARY/PREPAREDNESS ACTIONS...

When it is safe to do so, please report flooding or landslides 
threatening roads or property to the National Weather Service 
Greenville-Spartanburg by calling toll free, 1, 800, 2 6 7, 8 1 0 1, 
by posting on our Facebook page, or via X using hashtag NWSGSP. Your 
message should describe the specific location where impacts occurred 
and the depth of flooding observed.
