BULLETIN - EAS ACTIVATION REQUESTED
Flash Flood Warning
National Weather Service Greenville-Spartanburg SC
1203 AM EDT Thu Sep 26 2024

The National Weather Service in Greenville-Spartanburg has issued a

* Flash Flood Warning for...
  Southwestern Rutherford County in western North Carolina...

* Until 600 AM EDT.

* At 1203 AM EDT, Rutherford County Emergency Management is 
  reporting that the Town of Lake Lure has opened tainter gates to a 
  depth at or above 9 feet due to flash flooding above Lake Lure.  
  The resultant floodwave will impact several areas downstream, 
  including: River Creek Campground, homes in the Green Hill 
  community, areas in the Cleghorn and Union communities, and Cox 
  Rd.  Residents are urged to heed evacuation guidance from 
  Rutherford County Emergency Management and seek higher ground now.

  HAZARD...Life threatening flash flooding.  Torrential rainfall
           producing flash flooding resulting in dam gate
           releases.

  SOURCE...Radar.

  IMPACT...Life threatening flash flooding along the Broad River,
           impacting campgrounds, homes, roadways and other
           adjacent low-lying areas near the Broad River.

* Some locations that may experience Minor to Moderate flash 
  flooding include...
  Lake Lure, Harris and Cliffside.

PRECAUTIONARY/PREPAREDNESS ACTIONS...

Turn around, don't drown when encountering flooded roads. Most flood 
deaths occur in vehicles.

Be especially cautious at night when it is harder to recognize the 
dangers of flooding.

Flooding is occurring or is imminent. It is important to know where 
you are relative to streams, rivers, or creeks which can become 
killers in heavy rains. Campers and hikers should avoid streams or 
creeks.
