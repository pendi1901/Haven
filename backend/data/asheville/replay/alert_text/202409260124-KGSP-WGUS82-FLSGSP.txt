Transylvania NC-
924 PM EDT Wed Sep 25 2024

...FLOOD ADVISORY IN EFFECT UNTIL 9 AM EDT THURSDAY...

* WHAT...Flooding caused by excessive rainfall is expected.

* WHERE...Transylvania County, North Carolina.

* WHEN...Until 900 AM EDT Thursday.

* IMPACTS...Nuisance flooding of low-lying areas adjacent to streams 
  and other poor-drainage areas, including farmland, parks, 
  greenways, boat-access areas, golf courses, underpasses, and 
  parking lots.  Isolated, shallow flows over roadways is possible.  
  A few flood-prone, low-water crossings may become impassible.  A 
  small culvert washout or two is possible.

* ADDITIONAL DETAILS...
  - At 855 PM EDT, Doppler radar and automated rain gauges
    indicate Transylvania County has received 2-4 inches of
    rainfall in the past 3-4 hours.  This has resulted in
    significant rises on area streams, with the Davidson River
    exceeding Action/Advisory Stage causing nusiance flooding of
    adjacent low-lying areas.  Little River near Cedar Mountain
    and the French Broad River in Rosman are both approaching
    bankfull or Action/Advisory Stage as well.  These streams are
    expected to crest and begin to recede before the next round
    of heavy rainfall occurs.

    Heavy rainfall has become more intermittent across the
    county, but additional, more concentrated heavy rainfall is
    expected later tonight.  This will cause new rises on area
    streams and flash flooding is likely.  More significant flash
    flooding is likely later tonight into Thursday morning with
    repeated rounds of heavy rainfall.  Flash Flood Warnings are
    likely.  For your safety, avoid all low-lying areas near
    streams.  If you live or are vacationing near a stream
    prepare to seek higher ground now and avoid being caught by
    rapidly rising streams.  Do not wait for a Flash Flood
    Warning to take action to save your life.

  - Some locations that may experience nuisance to Minor flooding
    include...Brevard, Rosman, Dupont State Forest, Caesars Head
    State Park, Mount Pisgah, Connestee, Balsam Grove, Sassafras
    Mountain, Penrose, Cedar Mountain, Little River In
    Transylvania County, North Transylvania, and Sapphire.

  - Http://www.weather.gov/safety/flood

PRECAUTIONARY/PREPAREDNESS ACTIONS...

Be especially cautious at night when it is harder to recognize the 
dangers of flooding.

In hilly terrain there are hundreds of low water crossings which are 
potentially dangerous in heavy rain. Do not attempt to cross flooded 
roads. Find an alternate route.
