York SC-
230 PM EDT Sat Sep 28 2024

...FLOOD ADVISORY IN EFFECT UNTIL 4 PM EDT SUNDAY...

* WHAT...Flooding caused by excessive runoff is expected.

* WHERE...York County, South Carolina.

* WHEN...Until 400 PM EDT Sunday.

* IMPACTS...Minor flooding in low-lying and poor drainage areas. 
  Water over roadways.

* ADDITIONAL DETAILS...
  - At 230 PM EDT, Emergency management reported minor flood
    impacts along the Catawba River downstream of Lake Wylie, in
    eastern portions of York County. Minor flooding has been
    occurring since Friday afternoon and likely will continue
    into Sunday as runoff continues, and/or as water is moved
    through the chain of reservoirs along the upper Catawba
    River.
  - The Catawba River, and adjacent low-lying areas, are the most
    likely place to experience minor flooding. Buildings and
    parking lots will be flooded on Herrons Ferry Rd below Cherry
    Rd near Rock Hill. Significant flooding of Landsford Canal
    State Park is ongoing and the public is advised to avoid the
    area.
  - Some locations that will experience flooding include...
    Rock Hill, Fort Mill, Tega Cay, Newport, Lesslie, Catawba and
    areas immediately downstream of Lake Wylie.
  - http://www.weather.gov/safety/flood

PRECAUTIONARY/PREPAREDNESS ACTIONS...

Be aware of your surroundings and do not drive on flooded roads.
