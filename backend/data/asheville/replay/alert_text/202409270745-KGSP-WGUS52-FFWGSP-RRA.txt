BULLETIN - EAS ACTIVATION REQUESTED
Flash Flood Warning
National Weather Service Greenville-Spartanburg SC
345 AM EDT Fri Sep 27 2024

The National Weather Service in Greenville-Spartanburg has issued a

* Flash Flood Warning for...
  A Dam Floodgate Release in...
  Southern Rutherford County in western North Carolina...

* Until 930 AM EDT.

* At 345 AM EDT, Rutherford County Emergency Management is reporting 
  that the Town of Lake Lure has opened tainter gates fully due to 
  flash flooding above Lake Lure.  The resultant floodwave is 
  impacting several areas downstream, including: River Creek 
  Campground, homes in the Green Hill community, areas in the 
  Cleghorn and Union communities, Union Rd at NC 108, and Cox Rd.  
  Residents are urged to heed evacuation guidance from Rutherford 
  County Emergency Management and seek higher ground.

* Additional torrential, potentially devastating rainfall is 
  expected later this morning as Hurricane Helene moves across the 
  area.  There is the potential for another 3-5 inches of rainfall 
  across Rutherford County, with the highest totals along the Blue 
  Ridge Escarpment above Lake Lure.  This rainfall will occur on 
  saturated soils and excessive runoff into elevated headwaters 
  above Lake Lure will result in potentially catastrophic inflows 
  into the lake and downstream locations.

* If the rainfall forecast verifies, Major flooding will be likely 
  along and near Lake Lure and a Flash Flood Emergency may be 
  issued.  Residents are encouraged to heed guidance from local 
  officials and emergency management on any potential impacts to 
  property.  Drivers are encouraged to heed any barricades and avoid 
  all flooded areas.  This has the potential to be a historic and 
  extremely dangerous event.  Please stay weather aware and monitor 
  lake levels closely for any changes.

  HAZARD...Life-threatening flash flooding.  Heavy rainfall
           producing flash flooding resulting in dam gate
           releases.

  SOURCE...Dam operator.

  IMPACT...Life-threatening flash flooding along the Broad River,
           impacting campgrounds, homes, roadways and other
           adjacent low-lying areas near the Broad River.

* Some locations that may experience Minor to Moderate flash 
  flooding include...downstream of Lake Lure, Cleghorn community, 
  Union community, Green Hill community, Harris, and Cliffside.

Flooding is occurring along the Broad River.

PRECAUTIONARY/PREPAREDNESS ACTIONS...

If you are in low lying areas below the Lake Lure you should move to 
higher ground immediately.
