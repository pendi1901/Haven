...FLOOD ADVISORY IN EFFECT UNTIL 4 AM EDT SUNDAY...
...REPLACES FLOOD WARNING...

* WHAT...Flooding caused by excessive rainfall is expected.

* WHERE...Cabarrus County in the North Carolina Piedmont

* WHEN...Until 400 AM EDT Sunday.

* IMPACTS...Minor flooding in low-lying and poor drainage areas. 

* ADDITIONAL DETAILS...
  - At 801 PM EDT, gauge reports indicated heavy rain due to
    thunderstorms. Minor flooding is likely ongoing in the
    eastern portions of Cabarrus County along streams and
    tributaries of the Rocky River.  The stream gauge on the
    Rocky River below Irish Buffalo Creek reported that the
    stream heights were just falling into action stage,
    indicating nuisance flooding.
  - Flooding along Back Creek south of Harrisburg may be
    occurring near the intersection of Robinson Church Road and
    Stallings Road which is near Middleton and Rocky Meadows
    subdivisions.
  - http://www.weather.gov/safety/flood

PRECAUTIONARY/PREPAREDNESS ACTIONS...

When it is safe to do so, please report flooding or landslides 
threatening roads or property to the National Weather Service 
Greenville-Spartanburg by calling toll free, 1, 800, 2 6 7, 8 1 0 1, 
by posting on our Facebook page, or via Twitter using hashtag 
NWSGSP. Your message should describe the specific location where 
impacts occurred and the depth of flooding observed.
