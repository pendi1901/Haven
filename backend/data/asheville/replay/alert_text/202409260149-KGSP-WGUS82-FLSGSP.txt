Henderson NC-
949 PM EDT Wed Sep 25 2024

...FLOOD ADVISORY IN EFFECT UNTIL 930 AM EDT THURSDAY...

* WHAT...Flooding caused by excessive rainfall is expected.

* WHERE...Central and 

* WHEN...Until 930 AM EDT Thursday.

* IMPACTS...Nuisance flooding of low-lying areas adjacent to streams 
  and other poor-drainage areas, including farmland, parks, 
  greenways, boat-access areas, golf courses, underpasses, and 
  parking lots.  Isolated, shallow flows over roadways is possible.  
  A few flood-prone, low-water crossings may become impassible.  A 
  small culvert washout or two is possible.

* ADDITIONAL DETAILS...
  - At 929 PM EDT, Doppler radar and automated rain gauges
    indicated 2-3 inches of rainfall has occurred over the Mud
    Creek watershed over the past 4-6 hours due to multiple
    rounds of heavy rainfall.  This is causing Mud Creek to
    exceed Action/Advisory Stage, flooding the lowest elevations
    of William H King Memorial Park and approaching the lowest
    elevations of the Oklawaha Greenway.  At these levels, more
    expansive lowland flooding with nuisance impacts to adjacent
    farmland, golf courses, and nurseries is ongoing or has
    recently occurred upstream closer to Erkwood Dr.

    Heavy rainfall has become more intermittent across the
    county, but additional, more concentrated heavy rainfall is
    expected later tonight.  This will cause new rises on area
    streams and flash flooding is likely.  More significant flash
    flooding is likely later tonight into Thursday morning with
    repeated rounds of heavy rainfall.  Flash Flood Warnings are
    likely.  For your safety, avoid all low-lying areas near
    streams.  If you live or are vacationing near a stream
    prepare to seek higher ground now and avoid being caught by
    rapidly rising streams.  Do not wait for a Flash Flood
    Warning to take action to save your life.

  - Some locations that may experience nuisance flooding
    include...
    Hendersonville, Flat Rock, Etowah, Mountain Home, Laurel
    Park, Dupont State Forest, Jones Gap State Park, Valley Hill,
    Crab Creek, East Flat Rock, Tuxedo and Dana.

PRECAUTIONARY/PREPAREDNESS ACTIONS...

Be especially cautious at night when it is harder to recognize the 
dangers of flooding.

Stay away or be swept away. River banks and culverts can become 
unstable and unsafe.
