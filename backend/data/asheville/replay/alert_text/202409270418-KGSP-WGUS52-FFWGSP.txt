Flash Flood Warning
National Weather Service Greenville-Spartanburg SC
1218 AM EDT Fri Sep 27 2024

The National Weather Service in Greenville-Spartanburg has issued a

* Flash Flood Warning for...
  Central Burke County in western North Carolina...

* Until 215 AM EDT.

* At 1218 AM EDT, Doppler radar and automated rain gauges indicated 
  heavy rain falling across central Burke County. Gauges along Johns 
  River at Arneys Store indicate rapid stage rises. County 911 
  reported East Parker Rd in Morganton is closed due to water over 
  the bridge and water is flowing over Bost Rd and Spainhour Rd in 
  Morganton. Additional road closures are possible. Between 2 and 3 
  inches of rain have fallen. The expected rainfall rate is 0.25 to 
  0.5 inches in 1 hour. Additional rainfall amounts of 0.5 to 1 inch 
  are possible in the warned area. Flash flooding is ongoing or 
  expected to begin shortly.

  HAZARD...Flash flooding caused by heavy rain.

  SOURCE...Radar and automated gauges.

  IMPACT...Flash flooding of small creeks and streams, urban
           areas, highways, streets and underpasses as well as
           other poor drainage and low-lying areas.

* Some locations that will experience flash flooding include...
  Morganton, Valdese, Salem, Drexel, Glen Alpine, Table Rock, South 
  Mountains State Park, Lake James State Park, Lake James, Lake 
  Rhodhiss and Oak Hill.

PRECAUTIONARY/PREPAREDNESS ACTIONS...

Turn around, don't drown when encountering flooded roads. Most flood 
deaths occur in vehicles.

Be especially cautious at night when it is harder to recognize the 
dangers of flooding.
