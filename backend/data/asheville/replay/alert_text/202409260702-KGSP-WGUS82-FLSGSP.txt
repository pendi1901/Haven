...FLOOD ADVISORY IN EFFECT UNTIL 9 AM EDT THIS MORNING...

* WHAT...Flooding caused by excessive rainfall is expected.

* WHERE...A portion of Upstate South Carolina, including the 
  following counties, Anderson, Greenville and Pickens.

* WHEN...Until 900 AM EDT.

* IMPACTS...Minor flooding in low-lying and poor drainage areas. 
  Water over roadways.

* ADDITIONAL DETAILS...
  - At 301 AM EDT, Training thunderstorms have produced heavy
    rainfall across the affected area.  Minor flooding is ongoing
    or expected to begin shortly in the advisory area.  Between
    1.5 and 2 inches of rain have already fallen, and additional
    rainfall is on the way.
  - Additional rainfall amounts up to 1 inch are expected over
    the area. This additional rain will result in minor flooding.
  - Some locations that will experience flooding include...
    Anderson, Easley, Clemson, Pickens, Homeland Park,
    Powdersville, Central, Northlake, Pendleton, Liberty, Norris,
    Six Mile, Lake Keowee, Keowee Toxaway State Park, Table Rock
    State Park, Anderson Airport, Jocassee Gorges, Lake Jocassee,
    Lake Hartwell and Caesars Head State Park.
  - http://www.weather.gov/safety/flood

PRECAUTIONARY/PREPAREDNESS ACTIONS...

When it is safe to do so, please report flooding or landslides 
threatening roads or property to the National Weather Service 
Greenville-Spartanburg by calling toll free, 1, 800, 2 6 7, 8 1 0 1, 
by posting on our Facebook page, or via Twitter using hashtag 
NWSGSP. Your message should describe the specific location where 
impacts occurred and the depth of flooding observed.

Turn around, don't drown when encountering flooded roads. Most flood 
deaths occur in vehicles.

Be especially cautious at night when it is harder to recognize the 
dangers of flooding.
